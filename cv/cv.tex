\section{Academic Profile}
Ph.D. in Applied Sciences with a specialization in Mathematical Engineering and Mathematical Civil Engineer, specialized in the numerical analysis of partial differential equations. My research focuses on mixed and primal variational formulations, finite element methods in Hilbert and Banach spaces, nonlinear and coupled problems, poroelasticity, flow in deformable porous media, and a priori and a posteriori error analysis. It also addresses the establishment of existence, uniqueness, and stability of solutions at the continuous level and for semidiscrete and fully discrete approximations.
