The project develops and analyses mixed and mixed-primal finite element methods for nonlinear poroelasticity and related models, in which Biot's equations are coupled with nonlinear flow laws such as Brinkman and Forchheimer, and with transport or thermal effects.

  • Variational formulations in Hilbert and Banach spaces, with proofs of existence, uniqueness and continuous dependence on the data.
  • Discrete schemes that are stable and robust with respect to the physical parameters, in particular free of volumetric locking.
  • A priori and a posteriori error estimates, and adaptive algorithms based on the latter.
  • Numerical experiments motivated by applications in biomedicine and engineering.
